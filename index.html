Brief Bio

I am an Associate Professor at the National University of Defense Technology (NUDT). Previously, I held a Researcher (Type B Senior) position in the Department of Information Engineering at the University of Padova, which I took up in 2025. Prior to that, I was a Researcher (Type B Junior) in the same department from 2023 to 2024 and a postdoctoral researcher from 2022 to 2023. I was also a visiting scholar in the department from 2019 to 2021. I received my doctoral degree in Control Science and Engineering from Beijing Institute of Technology.

My research focuses on game-theoretic approaches to robust state estimation for uncertain dynamical systems. I also develop estimation and identification methods for tracking and navigation of unmanned systems, including unmanned surface vehicles (USVs) and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs).

Research interests: Robust estimation; identification theory; unmanned systems; signal processing; machine learning; deep learning.
